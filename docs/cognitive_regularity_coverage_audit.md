# Cognitive regularity and coverage audit

Date: 2026-09-11. Status: author derivations and scope audit; separate review pending. Target: H1/H2 of `smooth_observable_extension.md` for the approved gain-only, normal-working-effect cognitive pair. Verdict: likelihood smoothness is established; injectivity of the original parameterization fails on the specified design; a complete regular observable chart is NOT yet certified. The current design still gives identical S/K families, so it cannot yield strict superiority even if regularity is eventually certified.

## 1. Integrated likelihood is C-infinity locally

Write b_t=v_A,t A_t/2, d_t(rho)=v_A,t w(p_A,t;rho)-v_R,t w(p_R,t;rho), and use E~Normal(0,1):

    eta_t(E)=alpha+beta s_t+tau d_t(rho)
             -tau b_t(mu0+mu1 Z+sigma E),
    q(y|W;phi)=E_E product_t sigmoid((2y_t-1)eta_t(E)).

Take finite T and finite design support with finite payoffs/Z, tau,sigma,rho>0, all other parameters finite. Use log coordinates for the positive parameters if desired. No Gaussian assumption about TRUE context is used: the normal law here is the approved candidate working law.

On any compact neighborhood of an interior parameter, every parameter derivative of eta of order at most m is bounded by C_m(1+|E|). For a fixed design probability in (0,1), w is smooth in rho; at p=0 or 1 the stipulated function is constant in rho. Thus endpoints do not create rho-derivative problems. No derivative with respect to design probabilities is being asserted.

All derivatives of sigmoid of any fixed order are bounded: induction expresses them as a polynomial in sigmoid, whose values lie in [0,1]. Product and chain rules therefore bound each derivative of the participant integrand of order at most m by C'_m(1+|E|^m), locally uniformly in parameters. This is integrable against the standard normal density. Dominated differentiation proves C^m smoothness for every finite m, in particular C3. The use of a fixed standard-normal measure is essential; it avoids an unjustified derivative through a parameter-dependent density.

Every finite-parameter integrand is positive, hence every joint cell probability is positive. Continuity and the finite number of cells give a positive probability floor on each compact interior parameter neighborhood. This establishes smooth positive likelihoods and finite local score information. It does NOT establish nonsingular information, a complete chart, or a uniform floor over the unbounded global parameter space.

## 2. Original-parameter injectivity fails on the current support

The complete current design has Z in {-1,+1}, two trials, sides (-1,+1), b=(1/8,1/4), and d(1)=(-1/4,1/4). For any rho let d_1(rho)=a_r and d_2(rho)=e_r, and write

    D0(rho)=(a_r+e_r)/2, D1(rho)=(e_r-a_r)/2.

Since b_t=3/16+s_t/16, every logit reduces exactly to

    eta_t,z(E)=A+B s_t+c b_t z-lambda b_t E,
    A=alpha+tau D0(rho)-3 tau mu0/16,
    B=beta+tau D1(rho)-tau mu0/16,
    c=-tau mu1, lambda=tau sigma>0.

Thus the full observable law depends on only (A,B,c,lambda), regardless of whether rho is fixed or estimated. Conversely every such four-tuple is attained in S: take tau=1, mu0=0, mu1=-c, sigma=lambda, alpha=A and beta=B-1/4. The same is true in K by rho=1. These are surjective coordinates for both whole families; they preserve every shared term, rather than deleting nuisance effects.

S has six original parameters but its derivative rank is at most four; K has seven and rank at most four. Their original Fisher information matrices are therefore singular on this support. These bounds do not prove rank EXACTLY four. An inverse Hessian in the original parameters cannot verify H1.

This reduction also reproves Q_S=Q_K. Consequently all joint risk infima coincide for every DGP on this support. If regular observable charts exist, their tangent spaces coincide too and the theorem's added direction d is zero. More participants, a stronger optimizer or proper priors cannot create the missing population family difference.

## 3. Boundary escape can be excluded in the reduced coordinates near an interior law

Here b1,b2>0. Fix a law q0 generated by some finite (A0,B0,c0,lambda0) with lambda0>0. For each z, write m_tz=A+B s_t+c b_t z and pi_tz=E sigmoid(m_tz-lambda b_t E). Its two responses are positively associated, strictly below the maximal possible joint success probability:

    pi_1z pi_2z < q(1,1|z) < min(pi_1z,pi_2z).

The lower bound follows from

    Cov(f(E),g(E))=E[(f(E)-f(E'))(g(E)-g(E'))]/2>0,

where E' is an independent copy and both logistic functions are strictly decreasing for lambda>0. The upper bound follows because f,g lie strictly between zero and one: E[fg]<E[f],E[g]. These strict gaps remain positive for every law sufficiently near q0.

Consider any sequence of reduced parameters whose observable laws approach q0.

(a) lambda cannot tend to zero. Logistic is 1/4-Lipschitz, so Var(f_t(E))<=E[(f_t(E)-f_t(0))²]<=lambda² b_t²/16. Cauchy–Schwarz then bounds the joint covariance by lambda² b1 b2/16, contradicting its positive limit. This bound is uniform in m_tz.

(b) lambda cannot tend to infinity. Since each limiting marginal lies strictly in (0,1), the thresholds a_tz=m_tz/(lambda b_t) must remain bounded along such a subsequence: thresholds tending to plus/minus infinity would force the marginal to one/zero by dominated convergence. Select a further subsequence on which all thresholds converge. For E away from the finitely many limiting thresholds,

    sigmoid(lambda b_t(a_tz-E)) -> 1{E<a_tz}.

Dominated convergence makes the joint success probability converge to min(pi_1z,pi_2z), contradicting the strict upper gap of q0.

(c) With lambda bounded above and below, each m_tz is bounded. Otherwise a diverging m_tz forces its marginal to zero or one. Taking differences between the two z levels then bounds c, and the two opposite sides bound A,B.

Therefore every parameter sequence with laws approaching q0 has a subsequence converging to a finite reduced parameter with lambda>0, which represents q0 by continuity. The same argument applies to any law in a small neighborhood that retains the two strict dependence gaps. Limits of parameter values in that neighborhood are represented in the actual family: there is no additional zero/infinite-dispersion observable boundary stratum near q0.

This is a local compactness result for reduced parameters. It is stronger than merely ignoring divergent original parameters, many of which are redundant. It does not establish that different finite reduced parameters cannot represent q0 or nearby laws.

## 4. Exactly what remains for H1 and H2

To obtain a regular four-dimensional observable chart at q0 it remains to certify full derivative rank FOUR for (A,B,c,lambda) at the relevant preimage, and either uniqueness of the reduced representation of q0 or agreement of all its local image branches.

A usable sufficient route is:

1. Prove a nonzero 4-by-4 minor of the derivative of the six independent conditional-cell coordinates (three per z) at an explicitly specified baseline.
2. Prove the reduced preimage of that baseline is a singleton. Together with section 3, this forces all parameter representations of sufficiently nearby laws into a small neighborhood of that preimage: otherwise a contradicting sequence has a different limiting preimage.
3. Apply the inverse function theorem to the nonzero minor to construct a local embedding and continuous inverse. Section 3 excludes escaped representations; step 2 excludes other finite branches. These facts give H2 for the full observable closure near the baseline.

Neither rank-four certification nor uniqueness has been supplied by the present project artifacts. A grid of numerical Jacobian ranks cannot certify either condition. The audit therefore does NOT mark the cognitive H1/H2 gate passed. It also does not claim the reduced family is necessarily singular: failure of original parameter identifiability is distinct from failure of observable regularity.

The next constructive task is to specify a complete richer support under approved P-G1, preserving all shared terms, and test exact nuisance absorption before seeking observable rank and coverage. If the scientific goal instead requires a full regularity certificate on the current support, the missing task is explicitly the reduced-rank/unique-preimage proof above, not more original-parameter fitting.

The general smooth-family theorem remains a conditional theorem. This audit proves some of its application requirements and identifies precisely why the cognitive application is not yet verified. It establishes neither a cognitive positive KL gap nor Bayesian LOO convergence.
